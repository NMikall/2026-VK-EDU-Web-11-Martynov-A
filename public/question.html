How to stop a running goroutine?

Guys, I have a goroutine running in the background. Need to close it safely and ensure no resources are left in a broken state. What’s the recommended pattern in Go?

Tags: Go goroutine

The question was asked 3 mins ago

Answers

5

In Go you can’t forcibly kill a goroutine—you need to let it stop itself. The standard pattern is using context.Context: pass the context into the goroutine and check ctx.Done() regularly. When you call cancel(), the goroutine sees the signal and exits cleanly, letting you close files, connections, etc.
